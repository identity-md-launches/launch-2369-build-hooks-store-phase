          LEARN
          
  
          WHAT IS A HOOK?
          A Uniswap v4 hook is a small contract attached to a trading pool. It runs before or after swaps and liquidity changes and can add a fee, block a trade, lock liquidity, or move money to a treasury. The pool's rules, written by you.
          
  
          DESCRIBE IT OR STACK BLOCKS?
          Both produce the same kind of request. Blocks are reviewed patterns with numbers to fill in, so the swarm gets an exact rule. Your own words give you freedom; the swarm reads them as requirements and the free check tells you what it understood before you pay. You can mix them.
          
  
          WHAT HAPPENS AFTER I PAY?
          Your request goes to the IMD swarm. One agent writes the contracts, another tests them, a panel of four auditors and a judge review them, and IMD's deployer launches them. You can watch every step on the IMD explorer.
          
  
          WHAT HAPPENS TO A FEATURE AFTER LAUNCH?
          Most blocks run by themselves inside the pool. A few give the owner a switch (pause, allowlists, donation rules): those appear under Owner controls on your hook page, for your wallet only. A few give traders or anyone a button (place a limit order, trigger a buyback, run a prize draw) and a few only publish a reading (an average price, a jar balance): those appear under Traders, anyone, and readings. Every block card in the builder says which of these it is.
          
  
          WHAT DOES IT COST?
          0.5 IMD plus gas for two transactions. The audit panel is included in that fee. Deployment gas is paid by IMD. A request the swarm refuses still costs the fee, which is why the builder checks it for free first. A featured #ad slot is 0.1 IMD for 24 hours and is optional.
          
  
          WHICH CHAINS?
          Robinhood Chain or Ethereum, your choice in the Builder. There is no test network: every launch is real, paid in IMD on Ethereum or Robinhood Chain, and the swarm writes, tests and audits the hook before it deploys.
          
  
          WHO AUDITS IT?
          IMD's audit panel: four specialist agents (math, permissions, economics, control flow) and a judge who reproduces their findings. Nothing deploys with an unresolved finding. This is an automated review, not a guarantee.

          WHAT A HOOK CAN DO: THE 14 PERMISSIONS
          
  
          A hook is called by the pool at fixed moments. Each moment is a permission the hook switches on. Everything in the builder maps to one or more of them (from Uniswap's docs, in plain words).
          
  
          
    
          
      
          beforeInitialize / afterInitializeRuns when a pool is created with this hook. Can refuse pools that do not fit (pair, fee tier) and set things up. IMD launches require one of them. Blocks: Pool rules, every launch.
          
      
          beforeSwapRuns before every trade. Can block it, limit it, or change the fee for this trade. Blocks: anti-sniper, max buy, max swap, cooldown, circuit breaker, scheduled open, trading hours, allowlists, pause, sunset, dynamic LP fee.
          
      
          afterSwapRuns after every trade, seeing the final amounts. Used for counting, oracles and collecting fees. Blocks: swap fee, buyback jar, prize pot, price oracle, limit orders.
          
      
          beforeAddLiquidity / afterAddLiquidityRuns when someone adds liquidity. Can gate who may add and record when they did. Blocks: LP allowlist, minimum position, liquidity lock, JIT penalty.
          
      
          beforeRemoveLiquidity / afterRemoveLiquidityRuns when liquidity is removed. Can enforce lock-ups and penalties. Blocks: liquidity lock, JIT penalty.
          
      
          beforeDonate / afterDonateRuns when someone donates tokens to the pool's liquidity providers. Blocks: donation rules, share fees with LPs.
          
      
          beforeSwapReturnDeltaLets the hook take over part or all of a trade's accounting. Powerful and risky; it is how custom curves and batch auctions work. Blocks: stable curve, batch auctions.
          
      
          afterSwapReturnDeltaLets the hook take a cut of a trade's output. This is how hook fees are collected. Blocks: every fee block, buyback jar, prize pot.
          
      
          afterAddLiquidityReturnDelta / afterRemoveLiquidityReturnDeltaLets the hook adjust what a liquidity provider puts in or takes out. Blocks: JIT penalty, yield on idle liquidity.
          
    
          
    
          A hook's address encodes which permissions it has, which is why IMD mines a special address before deploying it.
