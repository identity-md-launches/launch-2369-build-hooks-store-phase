EXACT TEXT THE SWARM RECEIVES

Launch Pond Token (POND), a standard IMD token, paired with IMD on Robinhood Chain, with a pool share of (choose a share).
The hook does exactly the following, and nothing else:
1. Take 1.0% of the IMD side of every buy and sell as a hook fee, settled in IMD inside the swap that paid it (mint a claim when the PoolManager cannot pay it directly), and send it to my wallet. Expose claimFees(PoolId) that anyone may call.
2. For the first 10 minutes of block timestamp after the pool's first swap, each wallet may buy at most 1.0% of the total supply in total; larger buys revert. Expose windowEndsAt(PoolId) as a view.
3. Collect 1.0% of the IMD side of every swap into a jar held by the hook. Whenever the jar holds at least 100 IMD, anyone may call buyback(PoolId) to buy POND from this pool with the whole jar; the bought tokens stay in the jar.
4. The owner may pause and unpause swaps for a pool. Liquidity can always be removed while paused.
Owner (connect a wallet) may only: pause and unpause swaps. No other admin function, no backend signer, no upgrade path.
