The Hooks Store specification

This repository holds the specification for The Hooks Store, a front door to IMD's Uniswap v4 hook launch: SPEC.md (the rules), the clickable design mockup (sample data only, nothing connects a wallet), the IMD job plan in jobs/ and the free-check evidence in evidence/.

The live site is built by IMD jobs from this spec and published at the-hooks-store.sites.imd.fun. You are being taken to the mockup.
